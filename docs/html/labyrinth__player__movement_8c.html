Moves the player in the labyrinth according to the given direction.

Parameters
labyrinthPointer to the labyrinth structure.
dxHorizontal displacement (-1, 0, 1).
dyVertical displacement (-1, 0, 1).
rendererSDL renderer to update the display.
Note
If the player moves onto a key (KEY), they acquire it.
If the player moves onto a chest (CHEST), 1000 coins are added.
If the player moves onto a trap (TRAP), 500 coins are subtracted.
Movement blocked by a wall (WALL) or door (DOOR) is ignored.
